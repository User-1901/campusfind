The University Central Library provides an expansive collection of textbooks, research monographs, reference encyclopedias, and digital academic subscriptions (IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, SpringerLink, ScienceDirect).

Undergraduate students may borrow up to four physical volumes for a standard borrowing duration of fourteen calendar days, renewable once through the library portal if no holds are pending. Overdue items incur a fine of two rupees per day per book.

The Digital Reading Room is equipped with high-speed internet terminals for academic research and search engine indexing studies. Users must observe silence, carry college identity cards, and refrain from eating inside the library premises.
