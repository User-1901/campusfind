The Chief Warden Office outlines the mandatory residential regulations for all campus boarding hostels.

Hostel entry gates close promptly at 09:30 PM daily. Late entry without approved digital out-passes issued by wardens will trigger disciplinary notification to parents and guardians. Room allocations are non-transferable, and room inventory must be maintained in pristine condition.

Use of unauthorized high-wattage electrical appliances such as immersion heaters and induction stoves is prohibited to ensure residential fire safety. Campus security reserves the right to conduct periodic room safety inspections.
