Online enrollment for Open Electives and Departmental Honours tracks for pre-final and final year students is now active on the central academic portal.

Available interdisciplinary open electives include Cloud Computing, Financial Technology, Cyber Security Fundamentals, Sustainable Energy Systems, and Industrial Robotics. Seats across each elective course are capped at sixty students and will be allocated on a First-Come, First-Served basis subject to a cumulative grade point average threshold of 7.0 or higher.

The deadline for submitting course preferences and securing faculty mentor approval is August 22, 2026. Changes to selected elective papers will not be entertained after the add-drop deadline.
