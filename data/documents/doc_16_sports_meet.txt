The Department of Physical Education proudly announces the 28th Annual Inter-Collegiate Sports Meet and Athletics Championship, to be hosted at the University Sports Stadium from November 5 to November 8, 2026.

Competitive fixtures include Track and Field athletics, Football, Basketball, Volleyball, Badminton, Table Tennis, and Chess. Inter-departmental preliminary tryouts will take place between October 10 and October 15. Participating student athletes will receive special duty leaves for sanctioned competition matches.

Trophies, certificates of athletic distinction, and university sport merit scholarships will be awarded during the valedictory closing ceremony on November 8.
