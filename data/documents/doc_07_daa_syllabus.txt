Course Code: CS501 | Credits: 4 | Prerequisites: Data Structures and Discrete Mathematics.

Module 1 introduces algorithm analysis, asymptotic notation (Big O, Big Omega, Big Theta), recurrence relations, and Master Theorem. Module 2 covers Divide and Conquer strategies, analyzing Linear Search, Binary Search, Merge Sort, and Quicksort. Module 3 focuses on Greedy Methods, including fractional knapsack, Prim and Kruskal minimum spanning trees, and Dijkstra shortest path algorithm.

Module 4 explores Dynamic Programming principles, Matrix Chain Multiplication, 0/1 Knapsack, and Longest Common Subsequence. Module 5 introduces Backtracking, Branch and Bound, N-Queens problem, and NP-Completeness fundamentals. Textbooks: Introduction to Algorithms by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, and Stein (CLRS).
