This document delineates the standardized operating procedure governing answer script revaluation and supplementary examination applications following the announcement of semester results.

Students seeking verification or re-checking of their graded theory answer booklets may submit an online grievance form within fifteen business days of score publication. A non-refundable processing fee of five hundred rupees per course must be deposited via the campus payment gateway.

Supplementary and improvement examinations for candidates securing backlog grades will be scheduled concurrently during the winter inter-semester break. Students can register for a maximum of four supplementary course papers per examination cycle.
