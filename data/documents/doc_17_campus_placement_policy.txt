The Training and Placement Cell issues formal guidelines governing the 2026-2027 Campus Placement Season for all graduating engineering cohorts.

Eligibility criteria require a minimum aggregate CGPA of 6.5 across completed semesters, with no active academic backlogs at the time of recruitment registration. Students must register on the campus placement portal, upload an institutionally verified resume, and secure clearance from the dean of academics.

Under the university One-Offer Policy, once a candidate secures an offer with a compensation tier above eight lakhs per annum, they become ineligible for subsequent drives, ensuring fair placement distribution among registered candidates. Strict professional attire and code of ethics are mandatory during campus recruitment interviews.
