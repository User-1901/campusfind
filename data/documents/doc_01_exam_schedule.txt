All undergraduate and postgraduate students are hereby notified that the End-Term Theory Examinations for the Autumn Semester 2026 will commence on November 25, 2026. The examination session will run through December 14, 2026.

Examinations will be conducted in two daily shifts: Morning Shift from 09:30 AM to 12:30 PM, and Afternoon Shift from 02:00 PM to 05:00 PM. Detailed seating arrangements and room allotments will be published on the campus examination portal 48 hours prior to each scheduled examination date.

Students must carry their valid physical College Identity Card and printed Admit Card into the examination hall. Electronic devices including smart watches, programmable calculators, mobile phones, and wireless earphones are strictly prohibited inside the examination center. Any violation will be dealt with under institutional unfair means regulations.
