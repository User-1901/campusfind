The Academic Council publishes the comprehensive institutional calendar for the 2026-2027 academic year for all engineering and technology disciplines.

Autumn semester classes commence on August 3, 2026. The mid-semester internal assessment evaluations are scheduled from September 28 to October 3, 2026. The annual cultural and technological festival takes place in mid-October. Classes conclude on November 14, 2026, followed by practical laboratory exams from November 16 to November 21.

Spring semester registration commences on January 4, 2027, with regular classroom lectures starting on January 11, 2027. Students must plan internships, travel, and extracurricular commitments strictly in accordance with these gazetted dates.
