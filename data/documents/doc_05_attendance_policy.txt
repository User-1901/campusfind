---
title: Mandatory 75 Percent Minimum Attendance Policy
category: Academic Circulars
doc_type: Regulation
department: Dean of Academic Affairs
date: 2026-08-10
---
In accordance with statutory academic council ordinances, maintaining a minimum attendance of 75 percent in each enrolled lecture and practical laboratory course is mandatory for all students.

Attendance records are updated daily on the CampusFind institutional ERP portal. Students falling below 75 percent but having at least 65 percent attendance due to verified medical emergencies, hospitalization, or sanctioned inter-university sports representation may apply for medical condonation supported by registered medical practitioner certificates.

Students whose aggregate attendance falls below 65 percent will be debarred from writing the end-semester examinations and will receive a detained grade, necessitating course re-registration in subsequent academic sessions.
