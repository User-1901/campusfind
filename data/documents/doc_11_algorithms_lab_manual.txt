Laboratory Course Code: CS501L | Department of Computer Science.

Experiment 1: Implementation and empirical comparison of Linear Search and Binary Search on sorted text vocabularies. Students measure comparison counts, execution times using microsecond timers, and evaluate algorithmic asymptotic growth.

Experiment 2: Implementation of Divide and Conquer sorting algorithms: Quicksort and Mergesort. Experiment 3: Dynamic programming solutions to 0/1 Knapsack and Longest Common Subsequence. Experiment 4: Minimum Spanning Tree algorithms (Kruskal and Prim). All programs must be coded in Python 3 with modular docstrings, time complexity analysis, and verification against synthetic test datasets.
