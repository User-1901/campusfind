Applications are invited from eligible pre-final year students for the Summer Industrial Internship Drive with premier multinational technology firms, research labs, and financial institutions.

Recruiting partner corporations will offer paid internships spanning eight to twelve weeks during the summer term. Domains encompass Full-Stack Software Engineering, Cloud Architecture, Data Science, Cyber Security, and Embedded Systems. Selection pipelines include online coding assessments covering Data Structures and Algorithms, system design evaluations, and technical interviews.

Shortlisted students must obtain a formal No Objection Certificate from their respective departmental heads prior to commencing external internships.
