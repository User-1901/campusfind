The University enforces a strict Zero-Tolerance Policy towards ragging, harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any nature across academic, residential, and transport premises.

Ragging is a cognizable criminal offense under national education regulations and statutory penal codes. Any student found guilty of abetting or perpetrating ragging will face immediate suspension, rustication from the university, forfeiture of scholarships, and criminal prosecution before civil law enforcement authorities.

Victims or witnesses can report incidents confidentially to the 24/7 Anti-Ragging Helpline, the Proctorial Board, or via the anonymous grievance submission portal on CampusFind.
