The University invites registrations for TECHNOTRON 2026, the annual National Technical Symposium and Student Project Exhibition taking place on October 23 and 24, 2026.

Featured competitive tracks encompass Competitive Programming Contests, Paper Presentations on Machine Learning and Cloud Computing, Autonomous Robotics Mazes, UI/UX Web Design Challenges, and IoT Hardware Demonstrations. Technical workshops covering Large Language Model deployment and high-performance search architectures will be held by leading industry engineers.

Undergraduate and postgraduate students from accredited engineering institutions across the country are eligible to participate. Register via the campus portal.
